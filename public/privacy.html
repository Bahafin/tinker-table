Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version: Bumpity collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. What your child makes stays on your device.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Bumpity game: the web version and the apps for iPad and Android. Bumpity is made for children aged about 5 to 8, playing with a grown-up.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, an age, an email address, a photo, a location or any other personal information. It has no sign-in. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no code from other companies that could collect data.

The game never uses the camera, the microphone, contacts or location.

What stays on the device

To remember your child's play, the game keeps a few things in the device's own storage:

  • which levels are finished;
  • where stickers were placed in the sticker book;
  • machines kept in the toy box;
  • puzzles made in the puzzle maker;
  • the grown-ups' PIN, if you set one (kept only as a scrambled code, never as the digits).

None of this is sent anywhere. We can't see it. To remove it, delete the app, or clear the website data for the web version in your browser settings. Kept machines and made puzzles can also be deleted one by one in the grown-ups' corner, and progress and stickers can be cleared there too.

The voice

The game reads its own lines aloud (such as the goal of a level and the names of the rooms) using the speaking voice built into your device. Children never type or record anything. Some browsers offer online voices: if you choose one in your browser, the browser's maker turns the game's lines into speech under its own privacy policy.

Sharing a puzzle

A grown-up can share a puzzle made in the puzzle maker. Sharing is in the grown-ups' corner, which opens only with the grown-ups' PIN (or, until a PIN is set, a press and hold). The whole puzzle is packed into the link itself: only the parts and where they sit. The link holds nothing about you or your child, and no copy is kept on any server. You choose who to send it to, with your own messaging or email app.

Links and purchases

There are no links out of the game in the parts children play. The only link to this page is in the grown-ups' corner. There is nothing to buy inside the game.

Where the game is hosted

The store apps carry the whole game inside them and play without internet. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub may log technical details of each visit, such as the IP address, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. Apple and Google handle downloads from their stores under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. This is how it is designed to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the game ever needs to collect anything, we will say so here first and, where the law requires, ask a parent's permission first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email support@bumpity.app, or open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
