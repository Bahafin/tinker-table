What it is

Each puzzle is a little machine with a piece missing: a ball has to reach a basket, a sleeping hamster has to find his cheese, a rocket has to reach the stars. Your child adds ramps, springs, fans, magnets and dominoes, presses Go, and watches what happens. A friendly voice explains each puzzle, so there is nothing to read. A run that misses simply resets, ready to try again, and a faint ghost of a part helps when they're stuck.

The puzzle maker turns any machine into a puzzle to send to grandparents. It opens in any web browser.

Safe to hand over

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no data collected.
  • Works offline. Everything stays on the device.
  • A grown-ups' PIN keeps the settings and every link out of the game away from small fingers.
  • A play timer: after 15, 20 or 30 minutes the game falls asleep, and only a grown-up wakes it.
  • Step-by-step help to lock the iPad to the game with Guided Access.

For grown-ups

Every puzzle has a talk card in the grown-ups' corner: what it teaches, questions to ask, and something to try at home. Bumpity was made by a parent, for their own child, who playtested every room.
